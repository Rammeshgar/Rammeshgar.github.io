What the technical words mean
Saved rates / cache
Observations stored so the app can reuse them instead of asking again.
Rate source / provider
APILayer, the external service supplying exchange-rate observations.
Request / job
One task to obtain current rates, currency names or a range of daily history—not a visitor or a currency trade.
Waiting requests / queue
Data tasks waiting behind the one currently running.
Cooldown
A required pause before the app may try another request.
Fetch time vs observation time
When the app received data can differ from when the provider observed the rates.
