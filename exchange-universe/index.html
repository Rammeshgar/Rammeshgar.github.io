01 / The product

Make currency choices visible.

The original layout made it difficult to compare currencies and see their geographic context. I reorganised the app around Explore, Convert and Data.

01

Choose on the map.

Select a supported country to add or remove its currency from the comparison. The chart and map colours follow that selection.

02

Inspect the values.

Compare rates over time, convert an amount, or open the exact-value table and export a comparison as CSV.

03

Respect the API limits.

The app reuses saved rates and queues missing requests. Deduplication, bounded queues and retry backoff reduce unnecessary calls.

03 / One check in detail

Double the amount. Check the result.

05 / Decisions & limits

The part I am responsible for.

I built both projects with AI-assisted development, then reviewed the integration, user journeys and the evidence produced by the checks.

Why I chose the doubling check.

Checking the relationship between the two visible results avoids hard-coding an exchange rate that changes. It also exercises the input and reads the output the user sees.

What the checks cover.

Smoke verifies startup, chart data and conversion; Regression includes the atlas, comparison table and a real CSV export. I use the normal app controls and avoid the manual Refresh rates button.

What these results do not establish

Quotes are indicative, historical observations are daily, and availability depends on the API plan. The recorded monitor checks selected public user paths; they do not establish independent rate accuracy, service uptime or complete system coverage. The preview does not run the app or monitor.
